# Narmi

> Narmi is a digital banking platform for community banks and credit unions in the United States. The Narmi One platform unifies consumer and business digital banking, omnichannel account opening, instant payments over FedNow, fraud prevention, lending, an API-driven open architecture, and agentic AI capabilities. Narmi integrates with major core banking systems including Corelation Keystone, Symitar, Jack Henry Silverlake, Fiserv DNA, Fiserv Premier, FIS Horizon, FIS IBS, and COCC. Narmi customers have reported as much as 3x account growth in under 30 days and 4x deposit growth in under 90 days, and Narmi-powered institutions have won recognition including Bankrate's Best Online Bank and NerdWallet's Best Bank for Online Experience.

Narmi serves community financial institutions (FIs) ranging from small community to multi-billion-dollar regional institutions ). Customers include Grasshopper Bank, Arrowhead Credit Union, First Internet Bank, Citizens Bank of Edmond, Beacon Bank (formerly Berkshire Bank), Greater Alliance Federal Credit Union, FOCUS Bank, Middlesex Federal Savings, Meridian Trust FCU, Clark County Credit Union, Evergreen Bank Group, Outdoor Bank, Freedom Credit Union, New Cumberland FCU, University Federal Credit Union, LendingClub (formerly Radius Bank), TwinStar Credit Union, CTBC USA, North Brookfield Savings Bank, First Resource Bank, Magnolia Bank, Palisades Credit Union, Metro Credit Union, Savers Bank, PECU, WyHy FCU, Las Colinas FCU, Peoples National Bank, and others.

Narmi was an early mover on AI for community FIs, launching the first MCP (Model Context Protocol) server deployed by a U.S. bank — in partnership with Grasshopper Bank, in August 2025 — enabling business banking customers to query their financial data through LLMs including Claude and ChatGPT under OAuth 2.0–permissioned access. Narmi only leverages AI models whose providers agree not to train on institutional data; all agentic workflows ship with human approval checkpoints, full audit trails, and bank-grade security.

## Products

- [Narmi Banking](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-banking): Consumer and business digital banking — the everyday digital experience (web and mobile) for end users of a Narmi-powered financial institution. Includes account management, transfers, bill pay, card controls, A2A transfers with real-time core posting, and secure messaging.
- [Narmi Grow](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-grow): Omnichannel consumer and business account opening. Supports digital self-service account opening, in-branch staff-led account opening, and funding flows. Integrated identity verification stack via Alloy (decision engine), Socure (identity intelligence), and NeuroID (behavioral analytics). Used by customers to drive new account and deposit growth.
- [Narmi Command](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-command): Streamlined digital back-office for FI staff. Service portal that gives client-service and operations teams the tools to assist customers efficiently, including emulation, identity decisioning toolkit, and integrated CRM/ticketing.
- [Narmi Now](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-now): Real-time payments built on the Federal Reserve's FedNow Service. Narmi has full FedNow send-and-receive certification and went live with Grasshopper Bank in receive mode in August 2024.
- [Narmi Guard](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-guard): End-to-end fraud prevention suite securing the customer lifecycle. Defends against account takeover, transaction fraud, synthetic identity fraud, and other evolving threats across the digital channel.
- [Narmi Open](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-open): The API-driven open platform layer of Narmi One — public APIs, the Narmi Application Framework for embedding apps, and the AppXchange marketplace. Enables FIs to integrate third-party fintech apps and even agentic AI tools directly into the banking environment.
- [Narmi AI](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-ai): Agentic AI capabilities including the Narmi MCP server (enables end users to query financial data through LLMs like Claude and ChatGPT under OAuth-permissioned access), the Narmi AI search bar (conversational intelligence over banking data), AI-assisted marketing promotion generation, secure messaging assistance, and customer-service response acceleration. All workflows include human approval checkpoints, full audit trails, and bank-grade security. Models used do not train on institutional data.
- [Narmi Lend](https://www.narmi.com/narmi-lend): Modern, integrated lending platform for community banks and credit unions. Launched as part of the broader Narmi One platform to help FIs unlock new lending opportunities and meaningful growth.
